## Main findings

At fixed `N=429056`, changing FP64 residency leaves LU essentially unchanged
while strongly changing iterative refinement and therefore the final score:

```text
Anq       host GB   device GB   LU s    IR s   overall GFLOP/s
0          86.137     49.121    7.82    7.66    3.4031e+06
24576      66.450     68.808    7.76    5.14    4.0811e+06
49152      46.762     88.496    7.77    3.60    4.6299e+06
73728      27.075    108.183    7.77    2.36    5.1971e+06
98304       7.387    127.871    7.76    1.10    5.9457e+06
full fill   0.004    135.254   ~7.78    0.29   ~6.53e+06
```

LU throughput stays within about 0.75% across the residency curve. The final
score movement is therefore almost entirely explained by the IR term in:

```text
R_MxP ~= P_LU / (1 + T_IR / T_LU)
```

This cleanly confirms the campaign model: TASK-010 changed N and therefore
work plus residency together; TASK-2X8-011 holds work fixed and directly shows
that reduced FP64 device residency raises IR without materially changing LU.

The best partial-residency point (`Anq=98304`) is 8.90% below the stable
full-fill reference.
