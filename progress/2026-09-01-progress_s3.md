## Main conclusions recorded

- `--use-mpi-panel-broadcast` and `--u-panel-chunk-nbs` change communication
  and launch structure, but their clean performance differences are below the
  measured same-node drift for this single-node NVSwitch workload.
- `--prioritize-factorization 1` is the only retained phase-2 change. It cuts
  LU time from `20.38 s` to `19.12 s` and raises end-to-end GFLOP/s by `3.54%`
  against the same-job control.
- `--prioritize-trsm 1` is neutral, disabling the separate GEMM stream is a
  regression, and every tested nonzero DGEMV rows-per-thread value slows the
  iterative solver.
- The effective score is approximately `2.42e+06` GFLOP/s, about `+68%` over
  the original baseline. `dgemv_0` is the highest single observation at
  `2.4252e+06` (`+68.04%`).
