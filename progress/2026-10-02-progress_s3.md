## Mechanism reading

MPI/UCX is not small-message-only and is not broken here. Phase 4A and the
panel-10 diagnostic show healthy CUDA-aware rc_mlx5 with large-message
rendezvous zero-copy.

The key HPL-MxP semantic is that a positive panel percentage sends the **first**
X% of panel steps through MPI. Early HPL steps operate on the largest trailing
matrix, so small positive percentages assign MPI to the heaviest early panel
traffic.

HPL-MxP component tests strongly favor NCCL for U-panel broadcasts while the
smaller L2 gap is narrower. These pre-matgen component timings are supporting
mechanism evidence, not LU decomposition.

Likely contributors:
- NCCL is specialized/topology-aware for GPU collectives;
- better CUDA-stream collective scheduling/overlap;
- MPI/UCX remains GPU-direct but has less favorable collective/progress
  behavior for this exact panel critical path.

Positive policies also create a very different, more concentrated HCA traffic
shape, but whole-arm counters include the component tests. No fabric errors or
simple xmit_wait congestion signature was found.
