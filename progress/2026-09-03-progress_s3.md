## Main findings

- The baseline passes correctness but is **~44× slower** than the single-node
  8×H200 at comparable `N`/`NB`.
- Phase breakdown exposes the anomaly: `LU = 1634 s` (~27 min) dominates;
  matgen ~552 s, solver ~205 s. GPU utilization during the run was only
  0–26%, consistent with an inter-node communication bottleneck on the current
  container/pbsdsh bridge transport.
- RNG shows rank imbalance (`MAX 421 s` on g09 vs `AVG 282 s`), secondary to
  the LU cost.
