## Main finding

`--use-mpi-panel-broadcast 100` reduces mean NCCL broadcast-kernel time by
about 43%, but increases mean cumulative `MPI_Wait` time by about 23% and
stream-wait synchronization by about 19%. The dominant `nvjet` compute time
is unchanged. The profiled GFLOP/s increase is not attributable to the panel
policy because the solver phase and matrix-generation time differ materially
and the runs were on different nodes. The clean sweep supports retaining the
50% default/control.
