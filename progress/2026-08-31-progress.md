## Key findings

- Broadcast 100 reduces mean NCCL broadcast-kernel time by about 43% but
  increases MPI waits and stream synchronization; the dominant `nvjet` compute
  path is unchanged. The profiled score gain is not attributable to the panel
  policy, and the clean sweep supports retaining broadcast 50.
- Chunk 4 changes the execution schedule: the dominant `nvjet` launch count
  rises about 76% with essentially unchanged cumulative kernel time; NCCL
  broadcast launches rise about 6%; and recorded average collective
  synchronization is lower. The profiled score gain is not attributable to
  chunk size because the broadcast-100 profile has the same solver-time shift
  and the clean same-node chunk-4/chunk-8 results differ by less than control
  drift. Keep chunk 8.
