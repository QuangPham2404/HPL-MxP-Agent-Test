## Key finding

The 2x8 memory wall is the GPU HBM, not host RAM. At N=800000 all 16 ranks
abort with `cudaMalloc(&_mat_sp_dev, mat_sp_bytes) = 2 (out of memory)`
(matrix.cpp:298): per-process device consumption MAX 161.7 GB vs 138.7 GB
available per H200, while host RAM still had ~238 GB headroom per process.
Wall is between 700000 and 800000; implied device-matrix ceiling
N ≈ sqrt(139.8 GB × 16 / 4 B) ≈ 747000. The 1x8 host-RAM wall (510000 on a
single node) does not bind at 2x8 because the FP64 host matrix is split
across two nodes (~1845 GB/node used at N=700000).
