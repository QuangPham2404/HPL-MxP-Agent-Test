## What was completed

- **Plan agreed with user:** 5 attempts; fixed pristine trio g14+g16+g17
  for v1-v3 (and v5); opportunistic rotation for v4 (nearly-pristine g13
  used — no second pristine node existed); exp2 instrumentation reused
  (capture v2, 10 s sampler, UCX+NCCL logs, monitor-gpu); exp2's
  operational policy carried over (qdel pre-auth for own stuck jobs,
  pristine-loss re-pick rules).
- **Key design input from evidence:** exp2 showed PBS's pristine-node
  carve-out is deterministic (identical allocations across all 3 clean
  attempts), so exp3's fixed-trio arms measure determinism + the clean
  noise band rather than natural allocation variance.
- **Runs (all `PASSED`, N=250k, 3x4, exit 0):**
  - v1/v2/v3/v5.1 on trio (jobs 61022/61024/61026/61032): 70,792 / 71,367
    / 70,929 / 71,398 GFLOPS/GPU — 0.85% spread; +2019% to +2037% vs the
    original baseline.
  - v4 on g14+g16+g13 (nearly pristine, mixed-socket carve-out + one
    light co-tenant; job 61027): 68,385 GFLOPS/GPU (+1947%), −3.8% vs
    trio mean.
- **Failed attempt (recorded in README):** `cleanalloc250k_v5` (job
  `61031`) — accidentally submitted from the `outputs/` subdirectory →
  wrong `PBS_O_WORKDIR` → bridge-path resolution failed, exit 1 in ~1 s,
  no evidence. Stray `outputs/outputs/` PBS files removed after cause
  identification. Resubmitted correctly as v5.1.
- **Findings:** (1) pristine-node carve-out deterministic 5/5 and uniform
  across nodes (socket-1 GPUs `9A/BB/CD/DC` + socket-0 cpuset `0-47`
  every time) — no natural allocation variance exists on clean nodes;
  exp1's variance was co-tenant-occupancy-driven. (2) Fully-pristine band
  (70.8–71.4k GFLOPS/GPU) is the highest and tightest of the
  investigation. (3) Worst natural allocation shape (mixed-socket +
  fragmented cpuset, v4's g13) costs ≤~4%. (4) **Affinity-mismatch
  hypothesis closed as a major factor**: the fastest runs all used the
  mismatched default carve-out; remote-memory fractions 34–89% per node
  with no performance effect. (5) Per-node IB bytes identical across
  attempts (42.33–42.68 GB).
- **Track conclusion (exp1+2+3):** the 3x4 baseline's ~44x deficit =
  co-tenant host-contention dose-response (1.0x → 1.6x → 25x) times the
  comms-stack ceiling (container GPUDirect off — parent track); PBS
  placement/affinity is a minor (≤4%) factor. Guidance: pick nodes
  without active co-tenants; affinity tuning is low-yield until the
  comms fix.
- **Git:** commits `b28d77d`, `c0cc0f8`; GAAS re-synced (55 evidence files
  verified byte-identical, remote duplicates removed, `pull --ff-only`
  clean).
