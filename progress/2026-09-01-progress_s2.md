## Session results summary

| Experiment | Finding | Best / decision |
|---|---|---|
| `factorization-priority-test` | `--prioritize-factorization 1` is the only win (LU +6.6%, e2e +3.5%); `--prioritize-trsm` neutral | adopt factor 1, trsm 0 → new best `2.4203e+06` |
